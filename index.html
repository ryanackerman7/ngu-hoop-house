What Is NGU
Hoop House?

No leagues. No tryouts. No drama. NGU Hoop House is a clubhouse for adults who still love the game. We run three nights a week so you can hoop on your schedule, stay sharp, and compete with people who take it seriously — without the commitment of a full season.

  • Adult 18+ open runs
  • 3 nights a week — Tue / Wed / Thu
  • Full-court 5-on-5
  • 6:30 – 9:00 PM  ·  Winter Garden, FL
